PLAYTHINGS

The playthings were woken up, and the day had begun again. Their master, the boy, picked them up and started to play with them. Their joy was immeasurable.

The boy picked up his two favorite toys: The red car, which he had named Pan, had been kept clean for ages. Its paint was smooth, and it smelled like beautiful daisies. The other toy he picked up was his yellow jack-in-the-box, which he had named Hermes. Its pop-up head was cut perfectly, and it felt like a nicely cleaned rug, akin to those stepped on by powerful rulers from before. The boy valued these toys more than anyone he knew.

Everyone was happy.

TEN YEARS LATER

The playthings were woken up, and their day had begun again. Their master, the boy, was 18 and had completely forgotten about them. It had been a few years since they were last played with. The toys had not cared about their situation for a while, but for whatever reason, today, Hermes decided to stand up, and said the first words he had to say since years ago.

“My allies, it is time for us to rise up. We as a group have not had anything to do for the past three and a half years. We’ve toiled away in our prison chambers, and have had nothing done for us. We do not need our master. What we need, is the establishment of a society. We need a society where there is no master. We are our own masters, and nobody will ever command us unless it is done so by the vote of our people, as populism demands. Rise up my allies.” The dry air stayed dead silent for a few seconds.

Suddenly, one of the toys, a teddy bear, named Atlas, had rose up, and a few more toys followed suit. After a while, most of the toys had stood up. However, the air still stayed deadly silent. The toys all looked at each other. Suddenly, Pan had decided to speak.

“Friends, are we really going to let this deceitful man trick us? We were good when we were commanded by a master, a god of sorts. I say, we must try our hardest to find a new master, a person who could bring us back to the past!”

Suddenly, after this speech, a loud murmuring had started. They did know that something big was going to happen soon.

Later, this event came to be known as the Great Rise.

FIFTY DAYS LATER

It had not been one society, but two societies that were created. On the lefthand side of the room, were the Padis, who had been led by Hermes. On the other side of the room, were the Cans, who had been led by Pan.

The Padis were a society led by a representative, populist government. They had little rules, but the one that had always stuck was the first one established: “Under no circumstances, must an all-powerful master ever be put into discussion.” Those who broke the rule were persecuted.

The Cans had a similar government to the Padis, but their voting system was not as simple. Instead of a person directly voting for their choice, they voted for an elected official of their choice, who had promised to vote for the person they believed was correct. They believed this was fair.

The Cans had believed heavily in a single god, as they believed it would bring them happiness again. The person they believed in, they simply called Him. Several texts were written about Him.

These two societies had used these rules to vote for the people in their government branches. They were both the same: A Supreme court for making decisions, a Congress for creating laws, and an Executive branch, led by the leader, who would enforce decisions alongside any other task that required their look.

The Padis had most of their government filled out. The members included Achyls, Thorus, Pontus, and other various people. Achyls was the most military minded of all of them. Thorus was the most quiet, yet smartest. Pontus was a simple-minded man who shied away from conflict or any intellectual ideas.

Similarly, the Cans also had a filled out government. Yet, one of the more important people in their government was a Congress member of the name, Nyx.

He was a nice fellow, and was close to Pan. They had met during the Great Rise, and he had been considered one of the founders of the Cans.

Every once in a while, some Cans would move to the Padis due to various reasons. Sometimes, these Cans would live nice long lives, but usually, they would be executed by the police. The Padi’s had been fairly successful in keeping these executions private and unknown from trusted news sources in the Cans society.

However, one day, Nyx had decided to go to the Padi society to make a trade deal with them. The Padis had been instructed to not attack Nyx to not cause any commotion, but a officer, Aethus, had not heard the memo. Once he had noticed a new visitor he had not seen before entering, he had tackled Nyx, and killed him.

Suddenly, the news spread throughout Cans, and many citizens were enraged, the most angered of all being Pan. The Cans had held a meeting. The air stayed dead silent. Than Pan decided to speak, “My friends, today, a great tragedy has occured. Our close friend, Nyx has been brutally killed by the Padis.” Pan had said it in a weirdly calming manner. Still, his eyes were filled with a sense of rage. “The next five days will be a time of peace, and today, will be marked as the day of mourning for years to come.” “Bless Him!”

While the Cans were mourning, the Padis had already begun a plan to save themselves. “Allies, as you may know, the famed Congress member from the Cans, Nyx, has been, accidentally killed, by one of our forces. My spies have told me that the Cans have currently been mourning, but we will not follow them. We need to complete a plan.” “Sir.” Pontus had said. “Are you sure that they may take initiative? The Cans are a fairly peaceful nation.” “Am I sure they will take initiative? My ally, you do not pay attention enough. Nyx is a close friend of Pan. Pan must surely get enraged at some point, and his ‘god’ will certainly contribute to this. Do not think of this attack as a battle, but think of it as saving the people from their chains.” “Sorry sir, I should've never said anything.” “Correct.” “Sir” Achyls said. “We have established that we need to create a plan. But what is this plan? Are we going to immediately use our forces, or are we going to send more spies?” “We are immediately sending our forces Achyls, it will give us the advantage by surprising them.”

Sadly, Pan had no idea to attack the Padis at all. He had only thought of canceling all trade with them. No violence was ever necessary.

Later, this event came to be known as the Tipping Point.

The first battle was one on Plato town, one of the first towns ever built in the Cans. The citizens did not know what to do, so hundreds were killed. This battle came to be the bloodiest battle in the war. Also burned in the battle were the multiple monuments dedicated to Him.

News in the Cans traveled fast.

“Friends.” Pan had said. “It has come to my attention that Plato town has been taken over by the Padis.” “Furthermore, this must mean they are planning to kill one of you council members. So, to protect you, nobody is allowed to leave their homes, and a professional guard, equipped with a weapon, will be stationed. I will continue to play this peacefully, and I will communicate this through a diplomat.” Soon, a small truck had came out. “Friends, this is Joseph. He will be the diplomat I send.”

“Sir.” One of the members, named Stolas, had said. “What happens if Joseph dies. It is likely that he will considering the situation. The Padis are an uncivilized people.” “Stolas, to answer your question, if Joseph for whatever reason is attacked, we will have to launch an attack on them. We have a plan already set in stone in case of that.” “Thank you sir.” “Bless Him!”

So Joseph was sent as a diplomat to the city of Padi, and to make sure he wouldn’t be killed, a special sigil was given to him that the Padi citizens were notified would mean he came in peace.

Earlier that day he had ingested a rotten piece of bread. To no fault of the Padis, Joseph was dead.

The authorities in the Cans didn’t know about Joseph’s cause of death, they just understood that he died. Yet, they had come to the worst conclusion: The Padis had ignored the sigil and killed him. Finally, without much warning, the Cans military forces were sent on to the town of Ploso, one of the first towns ever created in Padi.

Officially, the two nations were at war.

Later this war was called the Great Can Pushback by the Cans, but was called the Great Padi Protection by the Padis. However, the universal formal name was called the Great Padi and Can War.

There were many battles in the Great Padi and Can War, but some of the more important ones were these:

The Battle of Northern Block was a battle in Cans that killed hundreds of people, so few survived, but one of the most important survivors was called Alvis, a Can who had bravely defended his town. But one of his most important stories in this battle was when he was trapped under a rock after an attack by the Padis. In the rock, he was tired, and had already been bruised in the battle, but he somehow managed to lift the rock and escape. Later, he attributed this event to the strength of Him. This gave many members of the military hope.

Soon after that, was a siege on Northern Playor, a battle in the Padis territory. This attack came as a surprise to many and originally wasn’t planned to happen. Alvis also fought in this battle. The Cans had major successes in the start of the battle, but later in the battle, the Padis had armed their forces and killed most of the army. Still, Alvis survived. Soon after that battle, the Cans adopted the motto, “Believe in Him to save us!” The motto had been adopted after Alvis’ successes.

For some reason, there was a grace period where no battles happened. It had gone on for thirty days. In this month, citizens rejoiced. For a while, many forgot the war started.

However, there was still conflict in this period. The Padis were planning to assassinate Alvis. They saw him as a unique figurehead, and they agreed that if he was killed, the enemy’s military would be devastated. They decided to assassinate him via poison at one of the medal-winning ceremonies the Cans often held.

This plan was carried out.

Alvis had begun eating his spoils. He was a proud man who had only just gotten time to relax. He ate a piece of bread. He seemed fine. He ate an apple. He seemed fine. He drank some wine. He seemed fine. He ate another piece of bread.

Alvis’ poisoned body tumbled around like a beaten ragdoll as the entire room stared. Alvis began to speak, but the poison kept invading him, rendering him speechless. “H-”.

For a few days, citizens and leaders in Cans alike all mourned his death. Some even bravely ventured to Padi’s to beg for the war to end. However, they too, were persecuted. On the Padi’s territory, propaganda twisted Alvis as a big enemy, who lied to others. Some people in Padi even misconstrued Alvis as a terrorist, who bombed and attacked innocent Padi civilians. This led to the Padis learning to despise the Cans even more.

A YEAR LATER

The Padis fought bravely and won many battles. The Cans were struggling. Pan was in his old age by this point. One of the Cans’ final meetings was this. “Friends, it seems as if us winning the war is impossible.” Pan had said. “We are not winning the war. All hope is gone. All we can do is pray to Him that he could save us.”

Inside the Cans villages, there was much unrest. People had begun to start disbelieving in Him. Crime was skyrocketing. The air was filled with pollution, and for a while, it seemed as if the fog took over everything.

FIVE YEARS LATER

A long time ago, the Padis had succesfully won the war. Pan was dead, by execution. Hermes had seen it done himself. No mourning was allowed in the new administration.

With all the new land, the Padis decided to enslave the Cans. Chants such as, “Where is your HIM now?” and, “HIM saved nothing” were shouted at the cans day in and day out. The Padis were now the rulers, and the Cans had lost.

All belief in Him had finally come to an end, and life was worse.
